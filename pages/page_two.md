# The Disadvantages of Open-Source Software

![Disadvantages of open-source software](./assets/images/open_source_disadvantages_illustration.png)

## Limited Technical Support

Open-source software also has disadvantages. Unlike commercial software, which may provide customer service, open-source projects often rely on online communities and volunteers. This can make it harder for beginners to solve problems. Responses may take time, and the quality of support can vary between projects. This is a disadvantage for users who need immediate technical assistance.

## Security and Maintenance

Public access allows people to examine code for weaknesses, but problems are not always fixed quickly. Small teams or limited resources can delay updates and security improvements. Users should therefore check whether a project is actively maintained and whether regular updates are available. Open access does not automatically guarantee secure software.

## Learning and Compatibility

Some programmes may be difficult for new users because their interfaces differ from familiar commercial software. Compatibility can also be an issue. For example, document formatting may change when a file is transferred between Microsoft Word and LibreOffice.
